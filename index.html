Charon
No evidence, no crossing.

AI agents grade their own work. They report "done" and "looks good" with equal confidence whether or not either is true. Charon is a skeleton for building gates made of numbers: a gate reads measured evidence, returns a verdict, and writes it to a tamper-detectable ledger. A verdict cannot be argued away.

Two application gates are included here, each motivated by a real failure from the author's own automated operation.

The Toll — five rules every gate obeys

  1. No evidence, no crossing: missing or unreadable evidence is HOLD, never PASS.
  2. A verdict reports what was measured, the threshold, and the source of the number.
  3. A verdict is bound to the SHA-256 of the artifact and the gate definition. Change either and the old verdict is void.
  4. The ledger is append-only and hash-chained. Tampering is detectable.
  5. Agents cannot overrule. Only a named human can record an override, and it stays visible as an override.
